# Valyrain Steel

A Minecraft Forge mod for 1.20.1 inspired by the weapons and equipment from *Game of Thrones* and *House of the Dragon*.

The idea is to bring some of the famous weapons from Westeros into Minecraft, with custom models and abilities for the Valyrian Steel weapons. It's still a work in progress, so some weapons are more finished than others.

## What's in the mod?

### Valyrian Steel weapons

The current weapon roster includes:

- **Dark Sister** – builds critical-strike chance with consecutive hits and has a charged dash attack.
- **Blackfyre** – a legendary sword with a flame-based ability. Still being worked on.
- **Catspaw Dagger** – can be thrown, launching a spectral copy of the dagger.
- **Longclaw** – included, abilities not implemented yet.
- **Ice** – included, abilities not implemented yet.
- **Calamity** – a large axe that can be charged and thrown, with a lightning effect on impact.
- **Nightfall** – included, abilities not implemented yet.
- **Tribute** – a castle-steel rapier.
- **Lady Forlorn** – has a charged wind burst and improves movement while airborne.

Some abilities may be unfinished or may not work exactly as intended yet. The descriptions above reflect the current state of the code and are subject to change.

### Other equipment and materials

- Castle Steel ingot and equipment
- Castle Steel sword, pickaxe, axe, shovel and hoe
- Castle Steel Plate armour set
- Iron Plate armour set
- Dragon Glass Shard
- Dragon Glass sword, pickaxe, axe, shovel and hoe
- Valyrian Steel Ingot

## Development status

This mod is still being developed. Not every weapon has its passive and active abilities finished, and some items may change as I work on them. Expect bugs and unfinished features.

## Requirements

- **Minecraft:** 1.20.1
- **Mod loader:** Minecraft Forge (47.x)

The project also uses GeckoLib and Lodestone for some of its custom item models, effects and animations. Make sure the required dependencies are installed when running the mod.
